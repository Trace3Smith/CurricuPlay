# Self-contained question audit

All 216 drafts were inspected. 49 explicitly requested teacher preparation/materials; another 7 depended on unstated classroom/learner-specific context or drawing materials. This conservative audit applies the rule that CurricuPlay supplies all task-specific information. Teacher reading, fingers/gestures, verbal reasoning, and complete example records displayed in a prompt do not require an external resource.

**56 flagged before revision; 37 replaced; 19 remain excluded.** Record count stays 216. No approvals were granted. Metadata and replacement wording invalidate previous local review fingerprints, requiring review under this new rule.

K/1 have sufficient category/difficulty coverage for a full board after balanced approval; grades 2–5 do not. A total of 30 alone does not guarantee category coverage. ## Replacement approach

Replacements preserve IDs, grade, subject, difficulty, standards/session, dates, and exact evidence. Only wording/answer and material classification change. Examples supply all relevant observations or location labels directly in the prompt; hypothetical records and pretend places are explicitly labeled. They are practice applications of a cited skill, not invented claims about real classroom observations, local geography, or historical events. No external visuals are required; short line-length examples are displayed as text inside CurricuPlay.

## Remaining evidence gaps

- Grade 2 Social Studies (2): objectives name President/Governor/Mayor but do not supply the role explanations needed for comparison.
- Grade 3 Social Studies (6): no usable in-app map or source-supported spatial definitions for the equator, prime meridian, latitude and longitude.
- Grade 4 Social Studies (6): topic/resource titles do not provide the historical people, actions, dates, events, and consequences needed to replace the taught-material tasks.
- Grade 5 Science (3): process names alone do not supply supported definitions or examples needed to classify/explain weathering, erosion, and deposition.
- Grade 5 Social Studies (2): source objectives do not supply the rights/responsibilities examples or distinctions needed for a fully checkable factual response.
