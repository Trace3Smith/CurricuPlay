## Grade 5

- **Social Studies, 1 pt:** Is obeying laws a citizen’s right or responsibility? → **A responsibility.**
- **Math, 3 pt:** A rectangular prism is 5 units long, 3 units wide, and 2 units high. Find its volume and explain. → **30 cubic units: 5 × 3 × 2 = 30. Each of two layers has 15 unit cubes.**
