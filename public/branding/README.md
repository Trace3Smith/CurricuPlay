# ClassThread branding

Official ClassThread logo (approved October 25, 2026). Source: "ClassThread Website Logo Kit".

- `classthread-logo-light.png` — horizontal logo for light backgrounds (teacher sidebar). Resized to 800px wide from the 2054px kit export.
- `classthread-logo-dark.png` — horizontal logo with a white "Class" for dark backgrounds (game headers). Resized to 800px wide.
- `classthread-icon.png` — book-and-figures mark alone, 256px wide.
- `favicon*.png`, `favicon.ico`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `classthread-app-icon-*.png` — browser and app icons, unchanged from the kit.

Do not recolor or redraw the logo. Do not place the light logo on the primary blue: "Thread" disappears.
